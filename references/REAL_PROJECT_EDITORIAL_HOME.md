Branch: design/czarno-bialy-podglad. Main remains untouched.

Structure follows the user reference: hero → four image tiles → expertise → existing interactive Poland globe → courses → enquiry form → social links → footer. All copy is Polish. Existing deep black, white architectural line traces, grain at hero edges and DM Sans typography remain.

Five public viewer-size images were downloaded without login, then encoded to WebP without modifying image content or colour. The public photo URLs are expiring; the website uses local copies. The four tiles are explicitly four views of this one project, not four different completed commissions. They open a native, keyboard-accessible five-image gallery. Image attribution and source links appear on the page and in the gallery.

The form retains its existing mail-app preparation workflow. Course names and descriptions come from content/products.json; no prices, lengths or new sales claims were invented. The 500+ figure remains explicitly marked as draft pending studio confirmation.

## Ambient grayscale / frosted offer refinement

Black-dominant fixed background blends diffuse grayscale and soft white light pools. The white line ornaments are retained at lower opacity. No coloured accents are introduced. Homepage hero occupies exactly 100svh, uses the authentic studio photo across the full frame, and has no top-left brand block.

The larger offer section now has three accessible linked glass cards: functional design, comprehensive design and author supervision. Frosted panels use backdrop-filter blur with a dark translucent fallback. A decorative low-opacity grayscale view of the studio project supplies depth behind the cards. The collaboration process remains a separate link below. Gallery, globe, courses, enquiry form and social links are preserved.
