## offer-functional-v1.webp

Plik: `dist/assets/offer-functional-v1.webp`. Rozmiar: 1122 × 1402 px.

Prompt końcowy:

Photorealistic natural architectural editorial photograph, portrait 4:5, for a large background service card on a Polish high-end interior design studio website. Subject: a carefully composed open-plan apartment threshold illustrating a functional interior layout, an elegant broad doorway with a dark walnut frame opening toward a quiet seating area, matte neutral taupe plaster, pale honed limestone floor, one built-in low walnut bench and soft natural daylight. Graphite, off-white, taupe, beige and dark walnut palette only, no orange or saturated accents. Calm expensive materials and precise geometry, real editorial architecture photography with straight verticals. Upper half shows architecture clearly; lower half is relatively uncluttered with darker neutral surfaces so white HTML text can be overlaid later. No people, no plants, no tools, no drawings, no letters, no logos, no UI. A conceptual interior, not a real client project.
